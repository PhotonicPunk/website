# Visibility Strategy Design
**Date:** 2026-03-09
**Goal:** Build consistent visibility with Atlantic Canada founders and the broader innovation ecosystem, generating inbound project-based consulting opportunities over time — without relying on social media.

---

## Context

- **Target audience:** Deeptech founders in Atlantic Canada, and other founders with similarly complex ventures
- **Engagement type sought:** Project-based consulting (founders typically reach out when struggling with a specific problem — technical, HR, funding, etc.)
- **Starting point:** Network partially re-engaged; no social media presence; blog live at johncormier.ca with one post published
- **Constraint:** No social media; distribution must rely on owned channels and borrowed audiences

---

## Pillar 1: Owned Content (Blog + Newsletter)

The blog at johncormier.ca is the permanent home for all writing. Every piece builds SEO and provides a shareable URL.

A Substack newsletter distributes new posts directly to subscribers. Write once; publish to blog and send to list simultaneously. Substack is free to start and handles subscriptions, delivery, and archives.

**Cadence:** One post every two weeks.

**Process:** Seed idea from existing topic list → collaborative development (hook, structure, draft, polish) → review → publish.

**Voice — two registers used naturally:**
- *Founder-to-founder:* Personal, specific, drawn from direct experience. Things only another founder would know.
- *Ecosystem observer:* Broader, analytical, zoomed out. For anyone with an interest in innovation.

---

## Pillar 2: Borrowed Audiences

Highest-leverage channel without social media. Write for publications and appear on podcasts that already have the target audience, with a byline pointing back to the blog and newsletter.

**Target outlets:**
- **Entrevestor** — covers Atlantic Canada startup ecosystem; read by founders, investors, ecosystem builders
- **Atlantic Business Magazine** — broader business audience; good for ecosystem-observer pieces
- **Ecosystem newsletters** — Volta, Venn Innovation, Ignite Fredericton, NBIF; guest contributions often welcomed
- **Podcasts** — Atlantic Canada startup/innovation podcasts as a guest; no writing required

**Approach:** Build a portfolio of 3-4 blog posts first, then pitch specific ideas (not generic offers). NBIF background and founder credentials are strong openers with editors.

---

## Pillar 3: Network Amplification

Network re-engagement (already underway) reinforces content efforts:
- Every coffee chat is a potential newsletter subscriber
- Every ecosystem reconnection is a potential introduction to an editor or podcast host
- Content gives conversations a concrete artifact to share; relationships give content reach

---

## Sequencing

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| Now | Write first 3–4 posts; launch newsletter on Substack |
| Month 2–3 | Pitch first guest contribution (Entrevestor or ecosystem newsletter) |
| Month 3–4 | Pursue first podcast appearance |
| Ongoing | One post every two weeks; one external pitch per month |
